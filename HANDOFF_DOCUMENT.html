1. Executive Summary & Architecture

The StreetSmart SOP QA System is an automated quality assurance evaluation engine and interactive management dashboard designed to audit, score, and provide actionable coaching for agency workflows across three core Standard Operating Procedures:

  1. SOP 1: New Business (Lead Qualification, 2-3 Market Quoting, CENT Phone Presentation, EFT Binding, SalesCenter).
  2. SOP 2: Policy Change Requests (Authority Verification, Category Question Guides, EZLynx Processing, 48h Follow-up, Carrier Download Match).
  3. SOP 3: Certificate of Insurance (COI) Requests (Written Request Intake, Red Flag Screening, Progressive LOB Breakouts, Dedicated Folders, Mandatory 7-Day Carlo Escalation Rule).

2. Standard Operating Procedures & 100-Point Rubrics

SOP 1: New Business

  • SLA: 5-min response, 2-hr prepare, 24-hr client delivery.
  • Non-Negotiables: NN_NB_01 Never email quote before phone presentation (Critical Fail); NN_NB_02 Do not request decs page at intake (Major Flag); NN_NB_03 EFT required before binding (Critical Fail).
  • 5 Categories: 1. Intake & Lead Qual (20 pts), 2. Quoting & Multi-Market (20 pts), 3. Phone Presentation & CENT (25 pts), 4. Won Policy & EFT (20 pts), 5. Follow-Up & Systems (15 pts).

SOP 2: Policy Change Requests

  • SLA: Same-day portal entry; 48-hour follow-up cadence until carrier download verification.
  • Non-Negotiables: NN_PC_01 Never close or confirm without verified carrier download (Critical Fail); NN_PC_02 Never bind agency-bill endorsement before payment received (Critical Fail); NN_PC_03 Never omit task/labels (Major Flag).
  • 5 Categories: 1. Verification & Core Intake (25 pts), 2. Branch Question Guides (25 pts), 3. EZLynx Processing & Billing (20 pts), 4. Follow-Up & Carrier Verification (20 pts), 5. Final Confirmation & Docs (10 pts).

SOP 3: Certificate of Insurance (COI) Requests

  • SLA: <1 hour turnaround from written request.
  • Non-Negotiables: NN_COI_01 Unsupported Proof (Critical Fail); NN_COI_02 Finance-Only Reinstatement (Critical Fail); NN_COI_03 Uncompleted Changes (Critical Fail); NN_COI_04 Carlo Escalation Failure for >7 Days Pending (Critical Fail); NN_COI_05 Oral-only Requests (Major Flag); NN_COI_06 Duplicate Holder Creation (Major Flag).
  • 5 Categories: 1. Intake & Written Verification (20 pts), 2. Coverage & Red Flag Screening (25 pts), 3. Master Preparation (20 pts), 4. Digital Delivery & Client Center (20 pts), 5. Tracking & Carlo Escalation (15 pts).
